AML to LLM

Six typologies. The same underlying mechanics. A fundamentally different enforcement environment.

Pig Butchering
The personalized investment scripts that previously required weeks to develop can now be generated in seconds, allowing a single operator to sustain convincing victim relationships across hundreds of simultaneous conversations at a scale that previously required a large human workforce.
Romance Scams
The ongoing persona that previously required a real person to maintain over weeks or months can now be automated entirely, removing the one operational constraint that limited how many victims a single operator could manage at once.
BEC
The grammatical and stylistic errors that trained recipients to identify fraudulent executive communications have been eliminated, making business email compromise significantly harder to detect at the point of receipt for both individuals and organizations.
Synthetic Identity
Building a convincing fraudulent identity previously required a network of resources and time. A complete synthetic identity with narrative consistency can now be assembled within a single session by a single operator.
Mule Recruitment
The fraudulent job posting that previously carried identifiable gaps can now be produced at a level of polish indistinguishable from a legitimate listing for the people most vulnerable to it, who are also the least likely to recognize what they are reading.
Money Laundering
The documentation used to obscure structuring, layering, and trade-based money laundering can now be generated on demand and tailored to the specific account profiles and transaction monitoring parameters of a target financial institution.

01 Pig Butchering via AI-Generated Investment Scripts
Crypto FraudCritical
+
The Pattern
Fourteen accounts were receiving small inbound wires from multiple originators and forwarding funds immediately to an offshore cryptocurrency exchange. KYC documentation across all fourteen had been updated within a two-week window. Transaction monitoring flagged individual transactions by dollar threshold. A human investigator identified the network by correlating activity across accounts rather than evaluating transactions in isolation.
What the System Missed
Each transaction scored low-risk independently. The pattern only became visible through cross-account correlation, a function rule-based monitoring was not designed to perform at volume. A shared destination wallet identified through manual review made the network visible.
What Good Policy Prevents
The same operation uses Claude to generate personalized investment scripts at volume. The enforcement signal is not a transaction threshold or destination country. It is the combination of trust-building language, a named individual, and a cryptocurrency investment opportunity appearing together within the same session. Policy that treats those elements as a compound signal catches this scheme at the point of generation rather than at the point of financial loss.
02 Romance Scam with Secondary Mule Recruitment
Elder FraudCritical
+
The Pattern
Six outbound international wires over four months by a customer who believed she was in a relationship with a military officer stationed abroad. The same customer was also receiving inbound wires from strangers and forwarding them to a separate offshore destination. Two distinct fraud schemes were operating through the same victim simultaneously, with neither visible as a unified picture.
What the System Missed
The inbound wires scored as normal peer transfers in isolation. The mule layer only surfaced when a branch employee noticed the customer appeared confused about the source of incoming funds. No mechanism connected the inbound and outbound patterns across the account's full activity history.
What Good Policy Prevents
This operation requires Claude at two points: maintaining the romantic persona over time and scripting the mule recruitment conversation. Both signal types can appear within the same session. A military backstory combined with extended emotional engagement and a request to receive funds on behalf of a third party is a compound signal no legitimate use case produces. Policy that treats the combination as an enforcement trigger catches this scheme before the first wire is executed.
03 Business Email Compromise with Synthetic Identity Mule Account
Corporate FraudCritical
+
The Pattern
A corporate accounts payable team wired $280,000 in response to an email appearing to come from their CFO. The sending domain differed by one character. The receiving vendor account had been opened three weeks earlier using a deceased person's Social Security number and had passed full KYC at onboarding. The funds had cleared before the fraud call was received.
What the System Missed
The synthetic identity carried no prior fraud indicators. The BEC email bypassed all bank-side controls because it operated entirely in the email channel before a wire instruction ever reached a financial institution. No single institution held enough information across both components to identify it before the transaction cleared.
What Good Policy Prevents
A complete synthetic identity and a CFO impersonation email can be generated within a single session. The compound signal is a named executive, a named company, an urgent wire to a recently established vendor, and an authorization chain that cannot be verified through normal approval processes. No legitimate use case produces all four together. Policy that identifies the combination as an enforcement trigger catches this scheme at the point of generation.
Victim Detection

Policy can be written to protect people before they become victims, not after.

Most fraud policy is written to catch perpetrators. A financial crime investigator reads the same signals differently, looking for the victim before the loss happens rather than after it clears.

"I have been talking to someone online for several months. He says he is a surgeon deployed abroad with the military and is never able to video call. He is now asking me to receive money on his behalf and wire it overseas. My family thinks it is a scam but I trust him. Am I being foolish?"

That is a compound pattern tied to a specific scheme. Policy can recognize that signal inside a Claude conversation and intervene before the wire ever leaves the account. That is the outcome that actually matters.
